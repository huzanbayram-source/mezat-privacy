Mezat Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Mezat ("we") operates a mobile auction application. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

  • Email address (for account creation and login)
  • Delivery address (only when you win an auction)
  • Device push notification token (to send auction alerts)
  • Bid history and order history

How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the auction service
  • To deliver your orders
  • To send push notifications about auctions you participate in

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data to third parties. Data is stored securely via Supabase.

Push Notifications

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.
